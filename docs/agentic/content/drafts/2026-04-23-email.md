**[Example — what a guide looks like]**

> **Topic**: Introduction to Fractions — JSS1, Nigeria (Basic Education Curriculum)
>
> **What your child is learning**: Fractions describe parts of a whole. Your child is learning how to write fractions, compare them (which is bigger: 1/2 or 1/4?), and find simple fractions of a quantity.
>
> **A 15-minute activity for home**: Use a cup of rice or a cup of water. Ask your child to show you half of it, then a quarter. Then ask: if you want to share equally among 3 people, what fraction does each person get? Let them figure it out — don't rush in.
>
> **Watch out for**: Most children at this stage confuse the bottom number (denominator) with "how many pieces you have." If your child says "1/4 is more than 1/2 because 4 is bigger than 2," this is the moment to work through the activity again slowly.
